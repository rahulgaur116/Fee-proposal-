# Request for Proposal — Owner's Representative Services

## Riverbend Unified School District — New Aquatic Center & Gymnasium

Riverbend Unified School District ("District") requests proposals from qualified
firms to provide Owner's Representative services for the design and construction
of a new aquatic center and gymnasium complex at Riverbend High School. The
program budget is $48,000,000, of which approximately $41,000,000 is
construction cost. The project is funded through a combination of the 2024 Bond
(Measure R) and a state grant.

The District intends to deliver the project via lease-leaseback. Design is
approximately 50% complete. Construction is anticipated to begin in summer 2027
and complete before the start of the 2029-30 school year.

## Scope of Services

The Owner's Representative shall:

1. Serve as the District's day-to-day representative and single point of
   contact for the design team, contractor, and District staff.
2. Manage the design process through completion of construction documents,
   including design review at each milestone and coordination of DSA approval.
3. Review and advise on the lease-leaseback agreements, preconstruction
   services, and the guaranteed maximum price.
4. Provide constructability review of the construction documents.
5. Manage the preconstruction phase, including bid package strategy and
   subcontractor prequalification support.
6. Provide construction phase oversight, including regular site presence,
   attendance at owner-architect-contractor meetings, and monitoring of the
   contractor's schedule and quality program.
7. Review contractor pay applications and make payment recommendations.
8. Review, negotiate, and make recommendations on change order requests.
9. Provide monthly written status reports to the District, and present to the
   Board of Trustees as requested.
10. Track the project budget, maintain a cost report, and administer the
    state grant reporting requirements.
11. Coordinate commissioning, punch list, and project closeout, including
    DSA closeout and grant closeout documentation.

## Proposal Requirements

Proposals shall include firm qualifications, proposed staffing, relevant K-12
experience (aquatic facilities preferred), references, and a fee proposal.
The fee proposal shall be a lump sum for the services described, with an
hourly rate schedule for additional services.

Proposals are due by 2:00 PM on October 15, 2026.
