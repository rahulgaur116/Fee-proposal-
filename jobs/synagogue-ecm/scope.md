# Scope of Work — Energy Conservation Measures (Concept Level Estimate)

Two buildings: Synagogue and Community House. Total approximately 100,000 SF
for both buildings. No other information available. Proposal is for a concept
level estimate only.

## Energy Conservation Measures

### Steam Distribution

- Reduce steam standby losses by reducing the extent of steam distribution,
  and convert all steam-fired units to hot water coils:
  - Option 1: convert to hot water centrally at the Synagogue and pipe across
    the street. Convert existing steam coils and piping to hot water. Option 1
    is more expensive than Option 2 due to the street excavation.
  - Option 2: convert to hot water at the Synagogue cellar and at the
    Community House cellar. Convert existing steam coils and piping to hot water.
- Upgrade from pneumatic controls at the PRV and main steam valves.
- Recover steam condensate and eliminate gravity return losses: centralizing
  the steam to hot water conversion will eliminate the gravity return
  inefficiencies and allow for central steam condensate recovery to domestic
  hot water or secondary heating circuit that has 24/7 load (e.g. perimeter
  AHUs at Sanctuary and fan coils at Community House circulate 24/7 in heating
  season). Reduce fan coil circulating temperature to receive the condensate
  recovery. Consider coil sizing and supply temperature required to achieve
  condensate recovery in the new selections.
  - Size replacement units and coils for 120F supply hot water temperature.
  - Reset supply hot water temperature to fan coils and AHUs.
  - Provide a condensate recovery tank at Sanctuary, pump set, and
    infrastructure to recover energy via hot water to Sanctuary perimeter
    AHUs, and a separate condensate recovery tank at Community House, pump
    set, and infrastructure to recover energy via hot water to Community House
    perimeter dual temperature loop.

### MEP Equipment

- Replace fan coils/thermostats and AHUs as per recommendations in FCA.
  - Typical (6) perimeter dual temperature fan coils per floor, 1 ton /
    400 cfm per unit.
- Convert from three-way valves to two-way valves at fan coils and AHUs.

### Ventilation and Controls

- BMS — fully integrate all new equipment and sequences.
- Implement demand control ventilation at the Community House — install VAV
  damper at outside air connection per floor and include demand control
  ventilation sensors.
- Retune off-hour controls to ensure the buildings are minimally conditioned
  overnight and off hours. Outdoor air should be reduced to code minimums.
- During off-hours at the Sanctuary, the interior AHUs should be shut down.
  The perimeter AHUs should run in minimum outdoor air mode. Demand control
  ventilation should be implemented with VAV box / outdoor air metering
  stations to achieve improved control rather than fully closed / fully open
  outside air dampers.
- Common exhaust duct — provide heat recovery at DOAS unit.
- Overnight shut-down of lobby units.

### Lighting and Electrical

- Lighting and controls — lighting in unrenovated spaces is fluorescent type
  without automatic control. Upgrade floors similar to 5th Floor Renovation with:
  - Approximately (30) 2x2 LEDs in classrooms, BOH, and offices.
  - Approximately (16) 5" recessed LEDs in corridors.
- Plug load controls — install duplex receptacle occupancy-based controls.

### Envelope

- Windows — replace windows in Community House with double pane IGU.
  - Alternate: replace with triple pane unit.
- Wall insulation — remove wall finishes during renovation of floor and add
  2" mineral wool. Replace wall finishes.
