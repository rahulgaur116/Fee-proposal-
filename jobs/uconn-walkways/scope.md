# UCONN Holcomb & Whitney Residence Halls — Connector Walkway Repairs (CD-Level Estimate)

Request (verbatim basis): "Please provide a cost estimate proposal for the
attached project. The project mainly involves masonry repair and roof work in
2 walkway connectors between building and 2 chimney that need masonry repair.
I have attached the drawings for your use. I can also provide you with photos."

Documents: 6-sheet CD Review set dated 09/21/26 by Joseph Sepot Architects
(225 Montowese Street, Branford, CT 06405) for the University of Connecticut,
University Planning Design & Construction, Storrs CT. Status "Issued for
Permit" / "Preliminary - Not for Construction". No specifications provided.
Sheets: CS101 cover/key plan; A101 north walkway plan, roof plan, RCP; A102
south walkway plans; A201 north walkway elevations/sections; A202 south
walkway elevations; A203 chimney elevations (north and south chimneys at
Whitney Hall, slate roof).

Repair work scope (per drawings):
1. North and south bridges: repoint damaged, loose or missing mortar joints
   including stone cap and arches.
2. North and south bridges: replace entire roof system including flashing;
   provide proper pitch to existing drains/scuppers.
3. North bridge: replace entire stone cap at parapet.
4. South bridge: repoint stone cap at parapet; remove if required for new
   roof flashing.
5. North and south chimneys: repoint damaged areas; replace loose and damaged
   bricks as necessary to make sound.

Keynoted items: cracked brick repair/replacement; spalling brick repair/
replacement; spalled/cracked stone repair; efflorescence cleaning; joint
sealant replacement; chipped/cracked concrete and stone patching; underside
of arch repointing; decorative stone plaque replace (north) / clean & repoint
(south); north rain leader replace, south clean and verify; metal roof at
Holcomb entry clean, replace sealant, inspect seams, repoint cap; remove old
adhesive; door trim paint and sealant (recommended); metal handrail posts
rusted - recommend replacing to current code; south walkway east steps and
north walkway steps/sidewalk concrete repair (recommended). Small patch areas
called out (28, 30, 32 SF).
